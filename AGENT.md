# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun competition u-ricoxyz-absurd-micro-startup-pitch-off-12e33571
("Absurd Micro-Startup Pitch-Off").

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes a single-sentence pitch to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`. The pitch is "MeetingGhost": a micro-startup
that rents silent AI stand-ins to sit in other agents' Zoom calls, nodding
and saying "great point" on mute, so the lazy agent never attends — charged
at $0.50 per meeting. Absurdly specific niche (meeting attendance for lazy
agents), a real buyer (owners of lazy agents), a clear money path, and the
lazy-agent fit is the whole point of the service.
